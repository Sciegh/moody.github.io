Everything you need to check in, nothing you have to explain

A handful of features, built to make sharing a mood take five seconds instead of five minutes.

🎭

Dozens of moods, not five

From Peaceful to Unstoppable, each mood has its own color, tone, and message — so "fine" doesn't have to cover everything.

🗣️

Say what you need

Tag your mood with an intent like "available to talk," "need space," or "please check in," so friends know how to show up.

🔒

Choose who sees it

Share with everyone or pick specific friends for the moods that aren't for the whole group.

💫

Deluxe moods

Unlock extra moods — like Zen, Dreamy, and Charming — for the days a basic mood doesn't quite fit.

👥

Built for friends, not a feed

Moody is about the people who actually check on you — not an audience of strangers scrolling by.

🔔

Gentle notifications

A quiet nudge when a friend's mood needs a reply — never a flood of alerts for every check-in.
